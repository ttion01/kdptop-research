## Key findings

1. Amazon Art & Photography had 18,345 books and estimated daily sales of 5,702 during October 24 to November 22, 2026. It scored 71, ranking 190th among 2,100 niches.
2. Amazon search demand for Art & Photography includes 93,740 searches for coffee table books and 51,418 for winnie the pooh book. The leading coffee table books search term generated 2,634 purchases.
3. Amazon Art & Photography had a median of 21 reviews, and 74.2% of sampled books had fewer than 50 reviews. Only 0.7% sold 100 or more copies per month.
4. The median price in Amazon Art & Photography was $19.99, and the median book length was 208 pages. Books priced at $20+ accounted for 9,086 titles in the sample.
5. Art & photography, adults scored 72 with 18,130 books, while Art & photography for women scored 66 with 526 books. Art & photography for seniors had the lowest median review count among the listed sub-niches, at 10.

## How big is the niche

Amazon Art & Photography included 18,345 books across Best Sellers and New Releases lists during October 24 to November 22, 2026. This comprised 14,923 Best Sellers and 4,530 New Releases. Print books accounted for 13,089 titles, while Kindle eBooks accounted for 5,256. Estimated daily sales across the niche reached 5,702 units. Its score of 71 placed it 190th among 2,100 niches, putting it in the stronger-scoring part of the market.

The niche covered 3,654 categories, including photography, architecture, art, travel, history, design, fashion and nature. The 93.4% KDP-feasible share indicates that most listed products use formats available to independent publishers.

## Competition and the review barrier

The sampled books had a median of 7 reviews, and 74.2% had fewer than 50. The review distribution included 9,820 books with fewer than 10 reviews, 3,802 with 10-50, 1,526 with 50-100, 2,295 with 100-500 and 907 with 500+. This distribution suggests that publishers can enter the niche without matching the review histories of established titles. Review counts alone, however, do not indicate likely sales.

Only 0.7% of sampled books sold 100+ copies monthly. The monthly sales distribution included 16,475 books below 30 units, 780 at 30-100, 101 at 100-300, 15 at 300-1,000 and 3 at 1,000+. Independently published books made up 24.7% of the sample, while 35.3% were published in the last year. The sample also included 1,322 newcomers and 1,108 breakouts, showing that new titles continue to enter the market and some gain traction.

## Should you publish in this niche?

Art & Photography offers a workable market for publishers able to produce visually distinctive print books around subjects with demonstrated search interest. The niche's score of 71, estimated daily sales of 5,702 and median price of $19.99 indicate an active market. The fact that 74.2% of sampled books had fewer than 50 reviews also suggests that a long review history is not a requirement for every title entering the niche.

Sales remain concentrated among a small share of books. Only 0.7% of sampled titles sold 100+ copies monthly, and independently published books accounted for 24.7% of the sample. Publishers should use the broad niche sales estimate as market context rather than as a forecast for a new release. Search data also includes both book-related and wall-art terms, making keyword relevance an important part of market research. A focused concept with a defined audience and a format suited to illustrated content has a clearer basis for entry. Generic titles face an established catalogue and limited evidence of consistent high-volume sales.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-22 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 18,345 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 18,350 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
