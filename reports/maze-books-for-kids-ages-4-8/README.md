## Should you publish a "maze books for kids ages 4-8" book?

The data supports a cautious yes for independently publishing a maze book for kids ages 4-8, provided the product has a clear point of difference. Estimated monthly searches reached 9,320 in October 2026. The selling-book sample had a median of just 1 review, and indie titles accounted for 92.0% of the sample. Competition across the wider market therefore appears accessible.

Sales concentration remains the main concern. Only 2.5% of selling books had estimated monthly sales of at least 100 units. The first-page print results had a median of 570 reviews, indicating that established titles can maintain strong positions. The $9.99 median price and 100-page median length offer useful benchmarks, but the small proportion of books with higher monthly sales means search demand alone does not justify publishing. ## Method and limits

Search volume, purchases and the monthly change are estimates for 2026-10. The selling books are those KDPTop saw in the last 30 days whose title has every word of "maze books for kids ages 4-8" (any order, singular or plural): 4,280 books. Reviews, sales, prices and title wording are counted on the 200 best sellers among those with a sales estimate, so the figures describe the top a new book stands next to, not every book of the kind. Sales are estimated from sales rank.

Title length, the counts a title states ("100 puzzles") and promise words are read off the titles by rule: they say what the title says, and nobody checked the book holds that many. "Title built as", "listing promises", "used for" and "likely buyer" are a language model's choices from fixed lists after reading 184 of the listings; the last is a guess.

The first search page is one reading of the page by KDPTop. Sponsored cards change with the time, the shopper and the delivery location, so their share is a sample of that one reading.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and sales-rank history; search volumes are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
