## Key findings

1. Amazon.com How-to guide lists contained 128,403 books as of November 22, 2026. Estimated daily sales were 36,295, and the niche scored 72.
2. Amazon search demand for How-to guide topics included 623,265 searches for scale and 621,519 for legos during the measured period.
3. The How-to guide market has a median of 23 reviews. A total of 73,650 books have fewer than 10 reviews, while 0.5% of sampled books sell 100+ copies monthly.
4. Independently published books account for 56.5% of the How-to guide niche. A total of 46.2% of sampled books were published in the last year.
5. How-to guide for seniors scores 78, with 3,458 books and a median of 20 reviews. How-to guide for nurses scores 74, with 1,166 books and a median of 10 reviews.

## How big is the niche

Amazon's Best Sellers and New Releases lists contained 128,403 How-to guide books as of November 22, 2026. The market comprised 78,577 print books and 49,826 Kindle eBooks across 7,770 categories. Estimated daily sales reached 36,295 units, reflecting demand across a wide range of subjects.

The niche scored 72 out of 100, ranking 155 of 2,100 niches. Strong demand and a relatively accessible review barrier support the score. However, the number of competing titles makes the broad How-to guide market difficult to target without a clearly defined subject.

## Competition and the review barrier

The sampled books have a median of 4 reviews. The distribution includes 73,650 books with fewer than 10 reviews, 22,970 with 10-50, 9,511 with 50-100, 15,984 with 100-500, and 6,269 with 500+ reviews. Overall, 75.3% have fewer than 50 reviews, so most competing titles have relatively modest review histories. Monthly sales are also concentrated at the lower end: only 0.5% of sampled books sell 100+ copies monthly, while 120,177 sell fewer than 30 monthly.

Independently published books account for 52.1% of the sample, compared with 56.5% in the overall niche statistics. Books published in the last year make up 46.2% of the sample. These figures point to substantial recent publishing activity, but the sales data shows that frequent publishing alone does not guarantee meaningful results.

## Should you publish in this niche?

The How-to guide niche combines estimated demand with a relatively accessible review barrier, making it worth investigating for independent publishers. Its score of 72 and indie share of 56.5% show that independent publishing has an established position in the market. Publishers with relevant subject expertise could examine narrower audiences such as seniors and nurses. These sub-niches score 78 and 74 respectively, with median review counts of 20 and 10.

The main concern is the difference between overall market demand and the sales performance of individual books. Only 0.5% of sampled titles sell 100+ copies monthly, and 75.3% have fewer than 50 reviews. A broad instructional title would compete with 128,403 books, while recent publishing activity already accounts for 46.2% of the sample. Publishers should evaluate the specific subject, competing titles, and category opportunity before committing to a book. The overall niche score does not predict the sales of an individual title.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-22 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 128,403 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 128,384 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
