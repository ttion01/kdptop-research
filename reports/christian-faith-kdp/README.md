## Key findings

1. As of November 22, 2026, the Christian & faith niche had 40,373 books across Amazon Best Sellers and New Releases lists, with 28,392 estimated daily sales. Its niche score was 68, placing it 371 out of 2,100 niches.
2. Amazon search demand for Christian & faith books is substantial. The keyword "bible" recorded 492,584 searches, while "bible study guide" recorded 151,669 searches in the reporting data.
3. The sampled Christian & faith books have a median of 9 reviews, and 64.9% have fewer than 50. However, only 3.1% of sampled books sell 100 or more copies monthly.
4. Independently published books make up 34.1% of the sampled Christian & faith market. Another 49.3% of sampled books were published in the last year, reflecting a steady flow of new releases.
5. New Year christian & faith scores 74, with just 6 median reviews across 181 books. Christmas christian & faith scores 73, with 40 median reviews across 1,061 books.

## How big is the niche

The Christian & faith niche has 40,373 books across Amazon's current Best Sellers and New Releases lists. This includes 26,600 Best Sellers and 16,121 New Releases. Estimated daily sales reach 28,392 units across the niche. Some books appear on both lists, so the individual counts should not be added to calculate the total number of titles.

The niche scores 68 out of 100 and ranks 371 out of 2,100 niches. Demand is its strongest scoring component, while the review barrier and price components also contribute positively. ## Competition and the review barrier

The sampled market has a median of 9 reviews, and 64.9% of books have fewer than 50 reviews. The distribution includes 20,362 books with fewer than 10 reviews, 5,843 with 10-50, 2,665 with 50-100, 6,292 with 100-500, and 5,211 with 500 or more. A large share of titles has relatively few reviews, although established books retain a considerable advantage. Only 3.1% of sampled books sell 100 or more copies monthly. Low review counts therefore do not necessarily indicate meaningful sales potential.

Independently published books account for 34.1% of the sample. Books published in the last year make up 49.3%, indicating a steady flow of new competitors. New titles can enter the market, but the small proportion reaching 100 monthly sales suggests that publishers need to choose their subjects and positioning carefully.

## Should you publish in this niche?

Christian & faith is a large KDP market, scoring 68 with 28,392 estimated daily sales. Independent publishers can find opportunities by targeting a defined readership and developing books around subjects such as Bible study, devotionals, prayer, or age-based Christian publishing. Search activity for terms such as "bible study guide" and "ethiopian bible" indicates that narrower subjects can generate substantial purchase activity. New Year christian & faith and Christmas christian & faith also have strong sub-niche scores relative to their review barriers.

The main concern is the gap between overall market size and the sales potential of an individual book. Only 3.1% of sampled books sell 100 or more copies monthly, and all the leading books in the table are non-indie. Nearly half of sampled titles were published in the last year, adding to the competition for new releases. Publishers are better served by selecting a specific subject and assessing its existing category competition than by entering the broad Christian & faith market with a generic title.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-22 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 40,373 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 40,373 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
