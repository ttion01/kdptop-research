## Should you publish a "i spy books" book?

The data supports a cautious entry into i spy books, with age-specific themes for children ages 3-5 or 8-10 offering potential starting points. Monthly searches reached 44,447, and independently published books accounted for 41.0% of the selling-book sample. The print first page presents a significant barrier: its 10 organic results had a median of 1,220 reviews, and none was independently published. An estimated 19.0% of selling books reached 100 monthly sales, suggesting that a relatively small group of stronger titles captures much of the demand.

This niche is suited to publishers who can produce illustrated seek-and-find books around a defined subject and age range. The $9.99 median price and 40-page median length offer practical reference points for product planning. Established print titles present the main competitive challenge. The 8-10 age term has fewer competing products than the leading preschool terms, while toddler-specific searches face substantial product competition. Publishers should weigh that competition before entering the toddler segment.

## Method and limits

Search volume, purchases and the monthly change are estimates for 2026-10. The selling books are those KDPTop saw in the last 30 days whose title has every word of "i spy books" (any order, singular or plural): 6,383 books. Reviews, sales, prices and title wording are counted on the 200 best sellers among those with a sales estimate, so the figures describe the top a new book stands next to, not every book of the kind. Sales are estimated from sales rank.

Title length, the counts a title states ("100 puzzles") and promise words are read off the titles by rule: they say what the title says, and nobody checked the book holds that many. "Title built as", "listing promises", "used for" and "likely buyer" are a language model's choices from fixed lists after reading 175 of the listings; the last is a guess.

The first search page is one reading of the page by KDPTop. Sponsored cards change with the time, the shopper and the delivery location, so their share is a sample of that one reading.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and sales-rank history; search volumes are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
