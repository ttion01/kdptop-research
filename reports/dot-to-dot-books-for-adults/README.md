## Key findings

1. Amazon.com recorded 12,089 searches for dot to dot books for adults in October 2026, followed by 1,423 purchases. Search volume fell 5.68% from the previous month.
2. Across the 200 selling books in the dot to dot books for adults market, the median review count was 3. A total of 79.5% had fewer than 50 reviews, suggesting that new listings can enter the market without first building a large review base.
3. Independently published books made up 90.0% of the 200 selling books for dot to dot books for adults. An estimated 58.4% had been published within the last year.
4. Selling dot to dot books for adults had a median price of $9.99, with the middle half priced between $9.99 and $11.99. The median page count was 105.
5. Relaxation is a common selling point in dot to dot books for adults. A total of 45.0% of titles explicitly use relaxing wording, while 25.0% specify a number of designs or puzzles.

## Method and limits

Search volume, purchases and the monthly change are estimates for 2026-10. The selling books are those KDPTop saw in the last 30 days whose title has every word of "dot to dot books for adults" (any order, singular or plural): 3,841 books. Reviews, sales, prices and title wording are counted on the 200 best sellers among those with a sales estimate, so the figures describe the top a new book stands next to, not every book of the kind. Sales are estimated from sales rank.

Title length, the counts a title states ("100 puzzles") and promise words are read off the titles by rule: they say what the title says, and nobody checked the book holds that many. "Title built as", "listing promises", "used for" and "likely buyer" are a language model's choices from fixed lists after reading 182 of the listings; the last is a guess.

The first search page is one reading of the page by KDPTop. Sponsored cards change with the time, the shopper and the delivery location, so their share is a sample of that one reading.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and sales-rank history; search volumes are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
