## Key findings

1. The Amazon.com search term how to draw books generated 12,548 estimated searches and 432 purchases in October 2026. Search demand peaks in November and December.
2. Among 200 selling how to draw books, the median review count was 282. As of November 26, 2026, 20.0% had fewer than 50 reviews.
3. Independently published books accounted for 36.0% of the 200 selling how to draw books. As of November 26, 2026, 24.6% had been published in the last year.
4. Selling how to draw books had a median price of $11.58. The middle 50% were priced between $9.99 and $14.96 as of November 26, 2026.
5. The related search term how to draw books for kids 8-12 recorded 28,906 searches and 1,789 purchases across 1,109 products. This gives publishers a more focused keyword target than the broad term.

## Should you publish a "how to draw books" book?

The data supports entering the how to draw books market with a defined audience, particularly children ages 8-12. The broad keyword remains competitive: the 200 selling books have a median of 282 reviews, and the leading print-book search results have a median of 1,700 reviews. Independently published books account for 36.0% of selling titles, indicating an established independent publisher presence in the category.

A children's drawing book with a substantial collection of clearly presented step-by-step projects would match the format used by leading titles. The median price of $11.58 and median length of 110 pages offer useful benchmarks. Publishers should account for the review advantage of established books, the lower indie share on the Kindle first page and the gap between searches and purchases among younger age groups. For example, the 4-6 age segment recorded 13,644 searches but only 74 purchases.

## Method and limits

Search volume, purchases and the monthly change are estimates for 2026-10. The selling books are those KDPTop saw in the last 30 days whose title has every word of "how to draw books" (any order, singular or plural): 11,338 books. Reviews, sales, prices and title wording are counted on the 200 best sellers among those with a sales estimate, so the figures describe the top a new book stands next to, not every book of the kind. Sales are estimated from sales rank.

Title length, the counts a title states ("100 puzzles") and promise words are read off the titles by rule: they say what the title says, and nobody checked the book holds that many. "Title built as", "listing promises", "used for" and "likely buyer" are a language model's choices from fixed lists after reading 197 of the listings; the last is a guess.

The first search page is one reading of the page by KDPTop. Sponsored cards change with the time, the shopper and the delivery location, so their share is a sample of that one reading.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and sales-rank history; search volumes are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
