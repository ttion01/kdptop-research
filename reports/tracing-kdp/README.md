## Key findings

1. The Amazon KDP Tracing niche contained 950 books across the Best Sellers and New Releases lists as of November 22, 2026. Estimated daily sales were 1050, and the niche score was 70.
2. The search term cursive handwriting workbook recorded 57056 searches and 15525 purchases during the measured period, making it the strongest term in the Tracing keyword data.
3. The Tracing niche had a median of 29 reviews. However, 70.2% of sampled books had fewer than 50 reviews, and 6.0% sold 100 or more copies monthly as of November 22, 2026.
4. Independently published books made up 77.9% of the Amazon KDP Tracing niche. A further 51.2% of sampled books had been published within the last year.
5. Tracing for stress relief scored 71 across 158 books, with a median of 12 reviews. Christmas tracing scored 66 across 38 books, with a median of 4 reviews.

## How big is the niche

The Tracing niche contains 950 books across Amazon Best Sellers and New Releases, comprising 625 Best Sellers entries and 374 New Releases entries. Print books account for 846 listings, compared with 104 Kindle eBooks. Estimated daily sales stand at 1050 units, with demand spread across handwriting, cursive, alphabet, lettering and calligraphy products.

The niche score is 70, placing it at 271 out of 2100 niches. Its demand component scores 0.916, while its review-barrier component scores 0.896. Both contribute strongly to the overall result. These figures point to an established market where publishers may find opportunities by developing books for specific uses and audiences.

## Competition and the review barrier

Review counts show that much of the market consists of books with relatively few reviews. Among 950 sampled books, 519 had fewer than 10 reviews, 148 had 10-50, 75 had 50-100, 105 had 100-500 and 103 had 500 or more. The sample median is 5 reviews, and 70.2% of books have fewer than 50 reviews. This leaves room for new publishers to compete for visibility, although established titles still benefit from their accumulated reviews.

An estimated 6.0% of sampled books sell 100 or more copies monthly. Independently published titles account for 78.0% of the sample, close to the niche-level figure of 77.9%. New releases also make up a substantial share of the market: 51.2% of sampled books were published within the last year. The data also identifies 94 newcomers and 49 breakout books. These figures point to regular market entry, although most individual titles sell fewer than 30 copies monthly.

## Price, page count and trim size

The median book price is $9.99. The 25th percentile is $8.50, and the 75th percentile is $12.00. The two largest price bands are $8-$10, with 293 books, and $10-$13, with 298 books. The median book has 106 pages. At 8.5x10.98, the dominant trim size is used by 601 books. The next most common sizes are 8.5x8.5, with 26 books, and 5.98x9.02, with 7 books. Most of the market therefore sits within a moderate price range and favors larger practice-book formats.

## Should you publish in this niche?

The Tracing niche has a score of 70, estimated daily sales of 1050 and an indie share of 77.9%. Search demand is strongest for cursive handwriting workbook and calligraphy practice book. Review counts are relatively low across much of the market, which gives new publishers some scope to compete for visibility. Several sub-niches are worth examining, including adults, stress relief, homeschoolers and specialist educational audiences. Tracing for stress relief scores 71, while Christmas tracing has a median of just 4 reviews.

The main challenge is the existing catalog of 950 books competing for broad tracing-related searches. Only 6.0% of sampled titles sell 100 or more copies monthly, and established children's handwriting books account for several of the highest estimated monthly sales figures. Publishers should also consider the market's established price range and dominant 8.5x10.98 trim size when planning a new title. The data supports developing a clearly differentiated practice-book concept, but it offers no guarantee that a new book will match the sales of established leaders.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-22 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 950 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 950 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
