## Key findings

1. Amazon.com searches for coloring book reached 418,326 in October 2026, followed by 73,082 purchases. Search demand fell 7.98% month over month, with the highest demand in Nov and Dec.
2. The 200 selling coloring books had a median of 954 reviews and median estimated monthly sales of 315 units. Just 5.5% had fewer than 50 reviews.
3. Print books made up 198 of the 200 selling books. The median price was $9.88, and the median length was 102 pages. An estimated 43.0% were independently published.
4. The first Amazon.com Print books search page had a median of 3268 reviews, compared with 25 for Kindle eBooks. On November 26, 2026, independently published books accounted for 20.0% of print results and 55.6% of Kindle results.
5. Related Amazon searches included dot by letter coloring book for adults, with 67,815 searches and 702 products, and anatomy coloring book, with 53,837 searches and 2688 products. Both terms had fewer competing products than the broad coloring book search.

## How hard is it to compete for "coloring book"?

The 200 selling books had a median of 954 reviews, and only 5.5% had fewer than 50 reviews. On the first Print books search page, the median was 3268 reviews, compared with 25 for Kindle eBooks. The difference points to a much higher review barrier in print. Independently published books made up 43.0% of the selling list, and 22.0% were published in the last year. An estimated 100.0% of the selling books sold at least 100 units per month, with median estimated monthly sales of 315 units.

The first Print books page contained 10 top organic books. Of these, 20.0% were independently published and 0.0% were published in the last 90 days. Estimated monthly revenue was $365919. Kindle eBooks also had 10 top organic books, with 55.6% independently published and 10.0% published in the last 90 days. Estimated monthly revenue was $1638. ## Should you publish a "coloring book" book?

Coloring book has high demand, but competition is substantial. The data favors an entry built around a defined audience or subject rather than a generic title. October 2026 recorded 418326 monthly searches and 73082 purchases, while the 954 median reviews and the 5.5% share of books with fewer than 50 reviews indicate that established sellers hold a strong position.

The market is better suited to publishers who can produce a print book with a clear point of difference in its audience, format, or subject. Related terms such as dot by letter coloring book for adults and anatomy coloring book have measurable demand alongside fewer competing products. Publication plans should account for the 102-page median, the $9.88 median price, and the concentration of sales among books with established review histories. November and December were the recorded peak months. The 7.98% monthly search decline also warrants careful consideration when choosing a launch date.

## Method and limits

Search volume, purchases and the monthly change are estimates for 2026-10. The selling books are those KDPTop saw in the last 30 days whose title has every word of "coloring book" (any order, singular or plural): 441,611 books. Reviews, sales, prices and title wording are counted on the 200 best sellers among those with a sales estimate, so the figures describe the top a new book stands next to, not every book of the kind. Sales are estimated from sales rank.

Title length, the counts a title states ("100 puzzles") and promise words are read off the titles by rule: they say what the title says, and nobody checked the book holds that many. "Title built as", "listing promises", "used for" and "likely buyer" are a language model's choices from fixed lists after reading 198 of the listings; the last is a guess.

The first search page is one reading of the page by KDPTop. Sponsored cards change with the time, the shopper and the delivery location, so their share is a sample of that one reading.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and sales-rank history; search volumes are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
