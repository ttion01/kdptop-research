## Key findings

1. Amazon.com Christmas book lists contained 6419 tracked books as of November 8, 2026, including 4561 Best Sellers and 3110 New Releases entries. Estimated daily sales were 21886.
2. Fiction led Amazon.com Christmas book sales by product type as of November 8, 2026, with 10890 estimated daily units across 1386 books. Picture books had the largest catalog, with 1449 titles.
3. Amazon.com Christmas book listings included 2198 books published in the last 120 days as of November 8, 2026. Titles older than one year accounted for 48.4% of books with publication dates.
4. Amazon.com Christmas best sellers included Cozy Girl Season at 8010 estimated monthly units and Baby's First Bible Stories Padded Board Book at 4920. The newest listed title, A Married Little Christmas, recorded 1980 estimated monthly units.
5. Amazon.com Christmas search demand included 338142 searches for christmas gift card book and 24240 searches for christmas coloring book. The latter recorded 114% reported search growth.

## Which product types sell

Picture books had the largest product-type catalog, with 1449 books, followed by Fiction with 1386. Fiction led estimated daily sales at 10890, while Picture book generated 2703. Other sizable categories included Cookbook with 549 books, Christian & faith with 443, and Adult coloring with 408. Games & trivia had a score of 76, while Kids coloring scored 77 and Sudoku & logic scored 79. The figures point to different publishing economics across formats. Fiction has the highest estimated sales volume, while activity and coloring formats account for a meaningful share of the catalog and have different price and review profiles.

Adults were the largest age group, with 3143 books and a score of 70. Among children's categories, ages 3-5 had 1248 books, ages 6-8 had 1031, and ages 9-12 had 417. Ages 13-17 had 287 books and a score of 73, while ages 0-2 had 293 and a score of 32. Publishers entering children's categories face an established catalog, particularly in the younger age groups. The age-band scores also indicate that a larger catalog does not necessarily correspond to stronger category performance.

## Backlist or new titles

Publication dates were available for 4704 books. Of these, 2198 were published in the last 120 days, representing 46.7% of the dated dataset. The older catalog included 229 books published 4-12 months ago, 451 published 1-2 years ago, and 1826 published over 2 years ago. Books older than one year represented 48.4% of dated listings.

The figures show that new Christmas titles continue to enter the market while older books retain substantial visibility. Almost half of the dated listings were published in the last 120 days, and nearly half were older than one year. The current breakout count of 1252 indicates that new books can gain traction during the season. Established titles, however, have the benefit of accumulated reviews and sales history. Publishers should examine competition within their niche and allow for the time needed to build reviews before committing to a late-season launch.

## When to publish

December is the peak month for Christmas books, with September through October identified as the recommended publishing window in the seasonal data. As of November 8, 2026, the peak is 1 month away. The main launch window has passed, leaving new titles less time to build ranking history and reviews before peak demand. Recent publishing activity remains substantial, with 2198 books published in the last 120 days.

A November launch can still be viable for formats that are quick to produce and have a clear seasonal purpose, particularly when the publisher has relevant keywords and a focused product concept. Existing titles can also benefit from the approaching December peak without requiring a new publication. Publishers planning a new seasonal book should compare production time with the remaining month before peak demand. A November release has less preparation time than a title published in September or October.

## Method and limits

Christmas books are books KDPTop's AI classification tags as Christmas-themed, counted on Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases lists in the three days to 2026-11-08. Publication age is the days from publication to 2026-11-08; monthly sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
