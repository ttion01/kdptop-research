## Competition and the review barrier

Review counts suggest a relatively low entry barrier. The median is 14 reviews, and 81 books have fewer than 10. The remaining review distribution includes 41 books with 10–50 reviews, 5 with 50–100, 4 with 100–500 and 1 with 500+. Across the broader sample, 92.4% of books have fewer than 50 reviews, indicating that most competing titles have yet to build substantial review counts.

Monthly sales are concentrated at the lower end: only 4.5% of sampled books sell 100+ units monthly, while 105 books fall into the under-30 monthly sales band. Independently published books account for 76.6% of the sample, compared with 71.4% in the niche statistics. Publishing activity is also recent. A total of 93.5% of sampled books were published in the last year, and the niche records 50 newcomers and 49 breakouts. New titles continue to enter the market, but relatively few sampled books reach substantial monthly sales.

## Should you publish in this niche?

Christmas sudoku & logic ranks 3rd among 1,605 niches, with a score of 78 under the supplied scoring model. Estimated daily sales are 274 units, the median review count is 14, and the indie share is 71.4%. These figures make the niche worth examining for independent publishers. Adults is the largest high-scoring sub-niche. Stress relief and seniors have lower median review counts and offer narrower positioning options.

The main concern is the difference between overall niche sales and the performance of individual books. Only 4.5% of sampled books sell 100+ units monthly, while 93.5% were published in the last year. New competition is entering the market regularly, but relatively few titles reach that sales level. The leading books also rely heavily on advent calendars, murder mysteries and escape-room formats, alongside sudoku. The absence of Amazon search-volume data makes it difficult to verify keyword demand independently. Publishers planning a seasonal release should also account for the September to October publishing window; November 2026 is already late for a full seasonal launch.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-08 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 132 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 132 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
