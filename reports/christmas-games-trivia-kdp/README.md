## Key findings

1. Christmas games & trivia ranks 6th among 1,716 Amazon KDP niches, scoring 78 and generating an estimated 1,403 daily sales as of November 15, 2026.

## Competition and the review barrier

The sample median is 10 reviews. A total of 211 books have fewer than 10 reviews, 140 have 10-50, 23 have 50-100, 31 have 100-500 and 25 have 500 or more. Overall, 81.6% have fewer than 50 reviews, indicating that new publishers can enter without matching the review counts of established titles. Sales are more concentrated: only 4.2% of sampled books sell 100 or more copies monthly based on estimated sales, while 361 sell fewer than 30 monthly. The typical book has 9 estimated monthly sales.

Independent publishers account for 74.0% of the sample, and 75.6% of books were published in the last year. Recent releases therefore make up a substantial part of the competition. The review barrier is relatively low, but the small share of books reaching 100 monthly sales shows that high-volume performance is limited to a minority of titles.

## Should you publish in this niche?

Christmas games & trivia scores 78 and ranks 6th among 1,716 niches in the dataset. The market is suited to independent publishers producing printable-style activity books, family games, age-targeted jokes and adult Christmas experiences. The 73.4% indie share and 21 median reviews show that independent publishers already have a substantial presence and can compete without exceptionally high review counts.

Sales concentration is the main concern. Only 4.2% of sampled books sell 100 or more copies monthly, and the median monthly sales estimate is 9. Established joke titles lead the top-selling list, while newer advent and interactive game formats have also gained traction with relatively few reviews. Publishers planning a release should work around the December peak and the September-to-October launch window. Given the November 15 data date, a new 2026 release would miss the preferred lead time.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-15 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 429 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 430 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
