## Key findings

1. Christmas games & trivia ranks 6th out of 1,715 niches, with a score of 78 and 441 books listed across Amazon Best Sellers and New Releases as of November 16, 2026.
2. Amazon's Christmas games & trivia lists record 1,412 estimated daily sales. The median book has 21 reviews and a median price of $9.99.
3. Independent publishers account for 74.3% of Christmas games & trivia books, and 75.4% of sampled titles were published in the last year.
4. Just 3.9% of sampled Christmas games & trivia books sell 100 or more copies monthly, while 80.3% have fewer than 50 reviews.
5. December is the peak month for Christmas games & trivia. September and October are the recommended months to publish.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-16 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 441 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 441 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
