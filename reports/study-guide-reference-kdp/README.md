## Key findings

1. Amazon.com Study guide & reference lists contained 78,428 books as of November 22, 2026, with estimated daily sales of 21,048. The niche scored 74, ranking 108th among 2,100 niches.
2. Amazon search data recorded 59,683 searches for dictionary and 20,220 for sat prep book 2026-2027. The dictionary keyword also generated 8,648 purchases.
3. Reviews present a relatively low entry barrier in Study guide & reference: 76.0% of sampled books have fewer than 50 reviews, including 42,590 with fewer than 10.
4. Only 0.5% of sampled Study guide & reference books sell 100 or more copies monthly. Independently published books account for 36.5% of the full niche, and books published within the last year make up 32.4% of the sample.
5. Study guide & reference for nurses scored 77 across 5,555 books, while Study guide & reference for seniors scored 76 across 403 books. Both sub-niches have median review counts below 25.

## How big is the niche

The Study guide & reference niche contained 78,428 books across Amazon Best Sellers and New Releases lists during October 24 to November 22, 2026. The total included 67,420 Best Sellers entries and 15,238 New Releases entries. Print books accounted for 54,073 listings, compared with 24,355 Kindle eBooks. Estimated daily sales reached 21,048 units across the niche, reflecting demand across a wide range of reference subjects.

The niche scored 74, ranking 108th among 2,100 niches. Strong demand, room for higher pricing and a relatively accessible review barrier support the score. The new-release inflow and breakout components are weaker, leaving room for improvement in those areas.

## Competition and the review barrier

Review counts suggest that many books in this niche face a relatively low barrier to entry. Of 78,428 sampled books, 42,590 had fewer than 10 reviews, 17,052 had 10-50, and 6,489 had 50-100. The median review count was 7, while 76.0% of books had fewer than 50 reviews. A large share of competing listings has therefore accumulated relatively little reader feedback.

Monthly sales are concentrated among a small number of titles. A total of 298 books sell 100-300 units monthly, 80 sell 300-1,000, and 15 sell 1,000 or more. The sample's indie share was 28.7%, compared with 36.5% in the overall niche statistics. Books published within the last year accounted for 32.4% of the sample, showing that new titles continue to enter the market.

## Should you publish in this niche?

Study guide & reference contains 78,428 books and has an overall score of 74, with estimated daily sales of 21,048. The fact that 76.0% of books have fewer than 50 reviews suggests that publishers can find subjects with a manageable review barrier. The strongest listed sub-niches are Study guide & reference for nurses and Study guide & reference for seniors, scoring 77 and 76 respectively. The nurses segment has a median price of $40.00, while the seniors segment contains just 403 books and has a median of 16 reviews.

Sales concentration is the main concern. Only 0.5% of sampled books sell 100 or more units monthly, and all of the top-selling books are non-indie. Publishers should evaluate demand by individual subject and keyword, since search volume and purchase activity vary sharply across terms. The overall niche score does not establish the sales potential of a particular study guide or reference subject. Category opportunity scores are also considerably lower than the overall niche score.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-22 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 78,428 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 78,428 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and sales-rank history; search volumes are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
