## Key findings

1. Kids coloring Christian ranked 7th among 1,716 Amazon KDP niches as of November 15, 2026, with a niche score of 78 and 127 estimated daily sales.
2. Amazon Best Sellers and New Releases lists included 359 Kids coloring Christian books as of November 15, 2026, with 118 Best Sellers and 274 New Releases list appearances.
3. The median review count for Kids coloring Christian was 3 as of November 15, 2026. A total of 322 books had fewer than 10 reviews, suggesting that new publishers can enter without a large review base.
4. Independently published books made up 86.4% of the Kids coloring Christian niche as of November 15, 2026. Also, 84.4% of sampled books were published within the last year.
5. The Amazon search term christian coloring book recorded 6,216 searches and 959 purchases. Sampled Kids coloring Christian books had a median price of $9.99.

## Competition and the review barrier

Review counts suggest that publishers can enter this niche without an established review history. Of the sampled books, 322 had fewer than 10 reviews, 17 had 10-50, 8 had 50-100, 6 had 100-500 and 7 had more than 500. In the 360-book sample, 94.2% had fewer than 50 reviews, and the median was 0 reviews. Sales were more concentrated: only 1.4% of sampled books sold 100 or more monthly units. A total of 334 books sold fewer than 30 monthly units, 21 were in the 30-100 band, 4 were in the 100-300 band and 1 was in the 300-1,000 band.

## Should you publish in this niche?

Kids coloring Christian is worth considering for independent publishers producing illustrated Bible content for children. The niche scores 78 and ranks 7 out of 1,716 niches. Its 86.4% indie share and median of 3 reviews indicate that publishers can compete without a substantial review history. Kids coloring Christian, ages 6-8 and ages 3-5 are the strongest age-specific options in the available data, with scores of 75 and 74 and median review counts of 2 each. Bible stories, Bible verses and Jesus-themed coloring already feature among the leading titles.

## Common questions

### Is Kids coloring Christian a profitable Amazon KDP niche?

The niche records 127 estimated daily sales and has a score of 78, ranking 7 out of 1,716 niches. Sales are concentrated among a relatively small group of titles, however. Only 1.4% of sampled books sell 100 or more monthly units.

### How many reviews do Christian children's coloring books need to compete?

The niche has a median of 3 reviews, and 322 books have fewer than 10. Across the broader sample, 94.2% have fewer than 50 reviews. These figures suggest that publishers can enter the market without first accumulating a large number of reviews.

### What is the best age group for Christian coloring books on KDP?

Kids coloring Christian, ages 6-8 scores 75 across 200 books and has a median of 2 reviews. Kids coloring Christian, ages 3-5 scores 74 across 107 books, also with a median of 2 reviews. Both segments offer a strong starting point based on the available figures.

### What price should I charge for a Christian kids coloring book?

The sampled books have a median price of $9.99, with a 25th percentile of $8.99 and a 75th percentile of $10.62. The median book is 66 pages long, and 8.5x10.98 is the most common trim size. These figures offer a useful reference when setting the price and format of a new title.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-15 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 359 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 360 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and sales-rank history; search volumes are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
