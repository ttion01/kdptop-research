## Key findings

1. Amazon.com recorded 26,972 searches and 863 purchases for toddler busy book in October 2026. Search demand fell 10.83% from the previous month.
2. The 43 selling books for toddler busy book had a median of 1 review in November 2026. A total of 88.4% had fewer than 50 reviews, suggesting that many tracked sellers have relatively short review histories.
3. Independently published books accounted for 78.0% of the 43 selling books for toddler busy book as of November 26, 2026. None of the tracked books had estimated monthly sales above 100 units.
4. Among priced selling books for toddler busy book, the median price was $9.99, with a P25 of $7.99 and a P75 of $16.99. The median book length was 80 pages as of November 26, 2026.
5. The related search term toddler busy book ages 1-3 recorded 6,386 searches and 175 purchases, with 1,788 competing products. Its search volume and smaller product pool make it a more focused targeting option.

## Should you publish a "toddler busy book" book?

The data supports a cautious entry into toddler busy book, with a defined age group or activity format offering a more practical starting point than a generic title. Independently published books account for 78.0% of the selling-book sample, and the median review count is 1. These figures show that independent publishers are active in the niche and that many tracked sellers have limited review histories. However, 0.0% of selling books reached estimated monthly sales of 100 or more units, while the main keyword returned 5,020 competing products. The 10 first-page print books had a median of 378 reviews, suggesting that gaining established organic visibility is harder than appearing in the broader selling-book sample.

Publishers considering this niche should have a specific toddler activity or sensory format and a clearly stated age range. The related term toddler busy book ages 1-3 has a smaller competing product pool, but its 1,788 products still represent substantial competition. Search volume for the main keyword fell 10.83% in October, so publishers should be cautious about assuming demand will increase. Estimated sales across the tracked selling books remain low, and the keyword data does not support an expectation of high sales volume.

## Method and limits

Search volume, purchases and the monthly change are estimates for 2026-10. The selling books are those KDPTop saw in the last 30 days whose title has every word of "toddler busy book" (any order, singular or plural): 575 books. Reviews, sales, prices and title wording are counted on the 43 best sellers among those with a sales estimate, so the figures describe the top a new book stands next to, not every book of the kind. Sales are estimated from sales rank.

Title length, the counts a title states ("100 puzzles") and promise words are read off the titles by rule: they say what the title says, and nobody checked the book holds that many. "Title built as", "listing promises", "used for" and "likely buyer" are a language model's choices from fixed lists after reading 33 of the listings; the last is a guess.

The first search page is one reading of the page by KDPTop. Sponsored cards change with the time, the shopper and the delivery location, so their share is a sample of that one reading.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and sales-rank history; search volumes are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
