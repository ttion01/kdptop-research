## Key findings

1. The Amazon KDP Cookbook niche contained 23,801 books across the Best Sellers and New Releases lists as of November 22, 2026. Estimated daily sales were 15,226 units.
2. The Cookbook niche scored 70 out of 100 and ranked 254 among 2,100 niches as of November 22, 2026, placing it in the upper tier of the measured market.
3. Amazon search data recorded 2,099,914 searches for bread and 908,094 for cookies. Both terms represent substantial search demand for cookbook publishers.
4. The Cookbook market had a median of 54 reviews. A total of 61.1% of sampled books had fewer than 50 reviews, while 44.7% had been published within the last year.
5. Christmas cookbook and Winter cookbook both scored 73, with median review counts of 26 and 19 respectively. Their review levels suggest comparatively accessible seasonal sub-niches.

## How big is the niche

The Cookbook niche contained 23,801 books across Amazon Best Sellers and New Releases lists during the October 24 to November 22, 2026 measurement period. Best Sellers accounted for 17,890 books, while New Releases accounted for 8,032. Print books represented 13,544 listings and Kindle eBooks accounted for 10,257. Estimated daily sales reached 15,226 units across the niche, showing substantial existing buyer demand.

The niche scored 70 out of 100 and ranked 254 among 2,100 measured niches. It received a full score for demand, while price opportunity and breakout potential scored more moderately. The market has enough volume to support specialized publishing. However, its overall ranking suggests that publishers should assess narrower subjects rather than approach cookbooks as one broad market.

## Competition and the review barrier

The niche had a median of 54 reviews, although the distribution shows that many sampled books had accumulated relatively few reviews. A total of 10,351 books had fewer than 10 reviews, while 4,186 had 10-50 reviews. Another 2,193 had 50-100 reviews, 4,156 had 100-500, and 2,914 had more than 500. Overall, 61.1% of sampled books had fewer than 50 reviews. This gives new publishers a sizeable group of competitors with limited review histories, although the market-wide median shows that established titles have accumulated meaningful social proof.

Only 2.3% of sampled books sold 100 or more copies monthly. The monthly sales distribution included 409 books in the 100-300 band, 97 in the 300-1,000 band, and 19 in the 1,000+ band. Independently published books accounted for 53.0% of the sample, and 44.7% of sampled books had been published within the last year. These figures show that independent publishers have an established presence in the market. However, achieving consistent sales at scale remains difficult.

## Price, page count and trim size

The niche median price was $11.72, with the 25th percentile at $4.99 and the 75th percentile at $17.76. The largest price bands were $13-$20, containing 6,162 books, and under $5, containing 6,378 books. Another 3,829 books were priced at $20 or more. Median book length was 168 pages. The most common reported trim size was 8.5x10.98, used by 2,796 books, followed by 5.98x9.02 with 1,199 and 7.99x10.0 with 437. The pricing and format distribution leaves room for lower-priced recipe collections as well as longer, higher-priced print cookbooks.

## Should you publish in this niche?

The Cookbook niche offers a viable market for independent publishers with a clearly defined subject and audience. Its score of 70, estimated daily sales of 15,226 and indie share of 53.0% in the sampled books point to an established market with substantial independent publishing activity. Seasonal subjects such as Christmas cookbook and Winter cookbook have relatively high niche scores. Cookbook for seniors and Cookbook for grandparents provide additional audience-specific options, both with lower median review counts than the broader market.

Sales concentration remains the main challenge. Only 2.3% of sampled titles reached 100 monthly units or more, despite substantial search interest in individual recipe subjects. Publishers also need to account for the 44.7% share of books published within the last year, which reflects continued competition from new releases. A focused cookbook concept backed by a demonstrable search term and a category with a comparatively high opportunity score has a stronger basis for entry. A generic cookbook without a distinct subject or target audience will have a harder time establishing its position.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-22 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 23,801 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 23,800 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and sales-rank history; search volumes are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
