## Key findings

1. The Amazon KDP Planner niche contained 1,165 books across the Best Sellers and New Releases lists from October 27 to November 16, 2026. Estimated daily sales were 418 units, and the niche score was 62.
2. Amazon search demand differs considerably by planner keyword. Wedding planner recorded 51,028 searches, compared with 4,510 for planner for men during the measured period.
3. The Amazon Planner niche has a relatively low review barrier. A total of 803 books had fewer than 10 reviews, and 85.7% of sampled books had fewer than 50 reviews.
4. Independently published books made up 79.7% of the Amazon Planner niche. A further 58.1% of sampled books had been published within the last year.
5. Planner for parents scored 66 across 134 books, while Planner for seniors scored 64 across 97 books. Both narrower markets scored above the overall Planner niche score of 62.

## How big is the niche

The Planner niche contained 1,165 books across Amazon Best Sellers and New Releases lists: 1,092 print books and 73 Kindle eBooks. Estimated daily sales were 418 units. The niche scored 62, placing it 373rd among 1,715 niches.

The market covers organization, planning, estate planning, end of life, wedding, scheduling, budgeting and final wishes. Its 826 categories reflect the range of planner formats and target audiences. The overall sales estimate, however, combines demand across a fragmented market.

## Competition and the review barrier

Most books in the sample had limited review histories. A total of 803 had under 10 reviews, 195 had 10-50, 58 had 50-100, 72 had 100-500 and 37 had 500+. The median review count was 2, and 85.7% of sampled books had fewer than 50 reviews. New publishers can enter a market where many competing titles have few reviews, although established books also have a presence.

Only 1.4% of sampled books sold 100+ copies per month, while 74.5% were independently published. Titles published within the last year accounted for 58.1% of the sample, with 195 newcomers recorded in the niche statistics. Most sampled books sold under 30 copies monthly, a group that included 1,086 titles. These figures suggest accessible entry conditions, but provide little evidence that most individual books reach substantial sales.

## Price, page count and trim size

The median Planner price was $10.99. The 25th percentile was $8.99, and the 75th percentile was $16.99. Listed prices covered six bands, ranging from under $5 to $20+. The median book length was 122 pages. The most common trim size was 8.5x10.98, used by 308 books, followed by 5.98x9.02 with 107 books and 4.02x6.5 with 33 books. This distribution accommodates compact planners at lower prices as well as longer formats at higher price points.

## Should you publish in this niche?

The Planner niche is worth investigating for independent publishers with a clearly defined audience or practical use case. Its score of 62, indie share of 79.7% and median review count of 8 indicate that independent publishers account for a large share of the market and that many competing books have limited review histories. Planner for parents, Planner for seniors and Planner for kids with special needs all scored above or close to the overall niche, giving publishers more focused markets to assess.

Sales remain the main constraint. Only 1.4% of sampled books sold 100+ copies monthly, while most sold under 30. Wedding planner recorded the highest search volume, but its 43,484 products point to substantial competition. Publishers also need to account for differences in price, format and target audience rather than treating Planner as one uniform product market. The data supports testing a well-defined planner concept, but does not show that a new title can match the sales of leading books.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-16 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 1,165 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 1,165 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
