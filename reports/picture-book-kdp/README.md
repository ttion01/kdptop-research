## Key findings

1. The Amazon.com Picture book market contained 28108 books across the Best Sellers and New Releases lists from 2026-10-27 to 2026-11-22, with estimated daily sales of 44207.
2. Picture book scored 69 out of 100, ranking 311 of 2100 niches in the KDP market during the October-November 2026 data period.
3. The niche statistics show a median of 29 reviews for Picture book. Some 70.5% of sampled books have fewer than 50 reviews, while 4.0% sell 100 or more copies monthly.
4. The Picture book market has a median price of $9.99, a median length of 33 pages and an indie share of 42.9%, according to the niche statistics.
5. Christian picture books score 78, picture books for parents score 75 and picture books for grandparents score 74. Their respective median review counts are 23, 19 and 12.

## How big is the niche

The Picture book market contains 28108 books across Amazon's current lists, including 16358 Print books and 13300 New Releases. Estimated daily sales total 44207 units across the niche. The market covers 3161 categories, with 1550 breakout books and 2126 newcomers recorded during the measurement period.

The niche scores 69 out of 100, ranking 311 of 2100 niches. Strong demand and a relatively low review barrier support the score, while the indie share and breakout performance indicate areas where the market could perform better. The 94.4% KDP feasibility figure suggests that most books in this market are suitable for KDP production.

## Competition and the review barrier

The niche statistics report a median of 29 reviews, compared with a median of 4 reviews in the sampled book dataset. The review distribution includes 15943 books with fewer than 10 reviews, 3859 with 10-50, 1605 with 50-100, 3575 with 100-500 and 3123 with 500 or more. Overall, 70.5% of sampled books have fewer than 50 reviews, so most listings have yet to reach higher review counts.

Only 4.0% of sampled books sell 100 or more copies monthly. The sample's indie share is 42.1%, close to the 42.9% reported in the niche statistics. Independently published books account for a substantial part of the market, although traditionally published titles remain prominent. Some 52.7% of sampled books were published within the last year, indicating a steady flow of new titles and continued competition from recent releases.

## Price, page count and trim size

The median Picture book price is $9.99. The 25th percentile is $4.99, while the 75th percentile is $12.99. Prices range from under $5 to $20+, with 6329 books in the $10-$13 band. The median length is 33 pages. The most frequently recorded trim size is 8.5x8.5, used by 5166 books, followed by 8.5x10.98 with 1250 and 7.99x10.0 with 849. ## Should you publish in this niche?

Picture book is a substantial market, with estimated daily sales of 44207 and a niche score of 69, placing it at rank 311 of 2100. Publishers with the ability to produce illustrated children's books may find it worth investigating, particularly if they can focus on a defined audience or subject. The sub-niche figures point to Christian themes, family relationships, grandparents and dog lovers as areas for further research. Their median review counts are below the broad niche statistics, which may give new publishers a more accessible starting point for competition.

Established titles present the main competitive challenge. The top-selling books have accumulated large review counts, and the leading examples are traditionally published. Only 4.0% of sampled books sell 100 or more copies monthly, despite the high estimated overall daily sales. Publishers should also consider that 52.7% of sampled books were published within the last year, adding to competition from recent releases. The data does not identify a seasonal peak for the overall niche, so these figures do not establish a specific seasonal launch window.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-22 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 28,108 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 28,105 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
