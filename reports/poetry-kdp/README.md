## Key findings

1. As of November 22, 2026, the Poetry niche included 8,681 books across Amazon Best Sellers and New Releases lists. Estimated daily sales were 3,289, and the niche scored 70, ranking 268 of 2,100 niches.
2. Amazon shoppers searched for the keyword "poetry books" 18,031 times during the measured period. The broader keyword "poetry" recorded 11,904 searches.
3. Review counts are relatively low across much of the sampled Poetry market. A total of 70.3% of books had fewer than 50 reviews, and the sample had a median of 5 reviews.
4. Independently published books accounted for 27.5% of the Poetry sample. A further 47.2% of sampled books had been published within the last year as of November 22, 2026.
5. Poetry for parents scored 68, with a median of 4 reviews across 82 books. Poetry, ages 3-5 scored 67, with a median of 8 reviews across 86 books.

## How big is the niche

The Poetry market covers 8,681 books across Amazon Best Sellers and New Releases lists, comprising 5,791 Best Sellers listings and 3,436 New Releases listings. Estimated daily sales total 3,289 units. The niche scores 70 out of 100 and ranks 268 of 2,100 niches, placing it among the stronger-scoring segments in the measured market.

The catalog includes 4,955 print books and 3,726 Kindle eBooks. With 99.9% of books classified as KDP-feasible, independent publishers have a broad range of potential print opportunities. However, traditionally published titles account for much of the visible competition.

## Competition and the review barrier

The sampled Poetry market has a median review count of 5. A total of 4,800 books have fewer than 10 reviews, 1,301 have 10-50, 627 have 50-100, 1,199 have 100-500, and 755 have more than 500. Overall, 70.3% of books have fewer than 50 reviews, so many listed titles have relatively short review histories. Estimated sales figures show that only 1.1% of sampled books sell 100 or more copies monthly.

Independent publishers account for 27.5% of the sample, and 47.2% of books were published within the last year. Sales remain concentrated among lower-volume titles: 7,736 books sell fewer than 30 copies monthly, compared with 434 in the 30-100 band, 72 in the 100-300 band, 17 in the 300-1,000 band, and 6 above 1,000. New books continue to enter the market, but relatively few achieve high monthly sales. A clear audience or subject focus is therefore important when developing a competitive title.

## Should you publish in this niche?

Poetry is a viable niche for independent publishers, scoring 70 with estimated daily sales of 3,289 and relatively low review counts across much of the sample. The sub-niche data points to three possible entry areas: Poetry for parents, Poetry, ages 3-5, and Poetry for men. Their scores range from 65 to 68, while median review counts range from 4 to 13. Publishers with a clearly defined audience or subject may find these segments easier to assess than the much larger Poetry, adults market.

The main challenge is the concentration of sales among a small share of books. Only 1.1% of sampled titles sell 100 or more copies monthly, and established books with substantial review histories remain prominent. Independent publishers also compete with a large body of traditionally published work, as shown by the 27.5% indie share. The market data supports considering a price near the $10.99 median and using established trim sizes. These benchmarks do not guarantee that a new title will achieve similar sales. The available data identifies no seasonal peak or recommended launch window for this niche.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-22 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 8,681 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 8,682 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
