## Key findings

1. Kids coloring, ages 6-8, scored 80 out of 100 and ranked 2nd among 1,605 niches as of November 8, 2026. The market includes 1,979 books across Amazon Best Sellers and New Releases lists.
2. Amazon search data for children's coloring books recorded 92,401 searches for "halloween coloring book" and 24,240 for "christmas coloring book" during the measured period, October 27 to November 8, 2026.
3. The Kids coloring, ages 6-8 niche has a median of 3 reviews. A total of 93.8% of sampled books have fewer than 50 reviews, and 84.1% of niche books are independently published.
4. The Amazon Kids coloring, ages 6-8 market has a median price of $9.99 and a median length of 71 pages. The most common recorded trim size is 8.5x10.98, with 551 books.
5. Kids coloring Christian, ages 6-8, scored 74 with 168 books and a median of 2 reviews. Christmas kids coloring, ages 6-8, scored 73 with 126 books and the same median review count.

## Competition and the review barrier

Most books in the market have few reviews. The median review count is 3. The distribution includes 1,732 books with fewer than 10 reviews, 126 with 10–50, 31 with 50–100, 49 with 100–500, and 42 with more than 500. Overall, 93.8% of sampled listings have fewer than 50 reviews.

Sales are more concentrated. The sample's median monthly sales are 5 units. Its monthly sales distribution includes 21 books selling 100–300 units, 5 selling 300–1,000, and 2 selling 1,000 or more. Only 1.4% of sampled books sell at least 100 units monthly. A low review count can make entry easier, but it does not necessarily translate into sales.

Independently published books account for 84.1% of the niche, while the broader sample has an indie share of 91.6%. Recent releases make up a substantial part of the market: 70.1% of sampled books were published within the last year. The data also records 129 breakouts and 134 newcomers, showing continued entry by new titles. Established books still account for most of the highest monthly sales estimates.

## Should you publish in this niche?

Kids coloring, ages 6-8, is a strong option for independent publishers. The niche scored 80, ranked 2nd among 1,605 niches, and has an indie share of 84.1%. Its median review count is 3, and 93.8% of sampled books have fewer than 50 reviews. These figures indicate that publishers can enter the market without facing a field dominated entirely by titles with extensive review histories. Christian, Christmas and Winter themes have particularly favorable combinations of niche scores and low median review counts.

The main concern is the difference between overall market activity and the sales performance of individual books. Only 1.4% of sampled books sell at least 100 units monthly, and the sample median is 5 monthly units. Established publishers and licensed properties hold several of the leading sales positions. At the same time, 70.1% of sampled books are recent releases. Publishers should consider how their chosen theme will compete with established formats and recognizable properties.

Search data shows strong interest in seasonal terms, but it does not identify the niche's seasonal peak or recommended publishing window. The available figures therefore do not support a specific launch-month recommendation.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-08 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 1,979 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 1,980 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
