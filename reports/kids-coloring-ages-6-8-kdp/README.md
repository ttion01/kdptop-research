## Key findings

1. Kids coloring, ages 6-8 ranks 2nd among 1,715 niches, with a score of 81 and 723 estimated daily sales as of November 16, 2026.
2. Amazon Best Sellers and New Releases lists contain 2,102 books in Kids coloring, ages 6-8. These include 706 Best Sellers and 1,528 New Releases.

## Competition and the review barrier

The sampled books have a median of 0 reviews, compared with a niche-level median of 3. Of the 2,102 books, 1,834 have fewer than 10 reviews, 140 have 10-50, 36 have 50-100, 45 have 100-500 and 47 have 500 or more. Overall, 93.9% have fewer than 50 reviews, which gives new entrants a relatively low review barrier.

Sales are more concentrated. Only 1.3% of sampled books sell 100+ units per month, while 2,013 sell fewer than 30 monthly units. Independently published books account for 91.1% of the sample, and 78.9% were published in the last year. Independent publishers make up most of the market, and new titles continue to enter. However, the sales distribution indicates that a small group of books accounts for most of the stronger commercial performance.

## Should you publish in this niche?

Kids coloring, ages 6-8 is a strong niche for independent publishers. It scores 81, ranks 2nd among 1,715 niches and has an indie share of 88.2%. The median review count is 3, and most books have fewer than 50 reviews. These figures suggest that publishers can enter the market without first building a review history comparable to established titles. Christmas, Christian and winter coloring have the strongest combination of scores and review barriers among the listed narrower markets.

Sales concentration remains the main concern. Only 1.3% of sampled books sell 100+ units per month, while 2,013 sell fewer than 30. Leading titles include established licensed properties and books with large review counts. A low review barrier therefore does not guarantee meaningful sales. Publishers should also factor in the $9.99 median price and the established preference for 8.5x10.98 formats when assessing production economics.

## Common questions

### Is kids coloring for ages 6-8 a profitable KDP niche?

The niche scores 81 out of 100 and ranks 2nd among 1,715 niches, with 723 estimated daily sales. However, only 1.3% of sampled books sell 100+ units per month. Overall demand therefore does not guarantee strong sales for an individual title.

### How many reviews do kids coloring books need to compete on Amazon?

The niche has a median of 3 reviews, and 1,834 of its 2,102 books have fewer than 10 reviews. In the sample, 93.9% have fewer than 50 reviews. This suggests that books can enter the market without an established review history.

### What is the best price for a kids coloring book on KDP?

The median price is $9.99, with P25 at $9.99 and P75 at $10.0. The largest price band is $8-$10, containing 1,189 books. The median page count is 69.

### Which kids coloring sub-niches have the best KDP opportunities?

Christmas kids coloring, ages 6-8 scores 76 across 157 books, with a median of 1 review. Kids coloring Christian, ages 6-8 scores 75 across 190 books, while Winter kids coloring, ages 6-8 scores 74 across 71 books. Both have a median of 2 reviews.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-16 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 2,102 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 2,102 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
