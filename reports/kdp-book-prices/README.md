# How Much Do KDP Books Cost? Price Benchmarks from 508,737 Best Sellers (November 2026)

> Source: KDPTop Research, KDPTop (kdptop.com). Original: https://kdptop.com/research/pricing/kdp-book-prices/

**Data window**: Nov 2, 2026 to Nov 8, 2026  
**Sample**: 508,737 books on Amazon.com Best Sellers lists

KDP Best Sellers pricing, estimated sales, Kindle royalties and KU, plus print prices by page count, based on 508,737 books from November 2–8, 2026.

|---|---|---|

## Key findings

1. Amazon Best Sellers covered 508,737 unique books from November 2–8, 2026: 287,153 Print books and 221,584 Kindle eBooks. Median prices were $14.99 for Print books and $6.99 for Kindle eBooks.
2. During November 2–8, 2026, 63.5% of Amazon Kindle Best Sellers were priced within the $2.99–$9.99 range eligible for the 70% royalty rate. Books priced under $1 accounted for 6.4% of Kindle eBooks.
3. The largest price band in Amazon Kindle Best Sellers was $5–$10, with 76,835 books representing 34.7% of the store during November 2–8, 2026. The under $1 band had the highest median estimated monthly sales at 9 units.
4. The largest price band in Amazon Print Best Sellers was $10–$15, with 68,356 books representing 23.8% of the store during November 2–8, 2026. The under $7 band had the highest median estimated monthly sales at 9 units.
5. Median prices in Amazon Print Best Sellers ranged from $9.99 for books under 50 pages to $24.99 for books with 400+ pages during November 2–8, 2026. The 250–399-page category contained 32,127 books.

## Print and Kindle prices

Amazon's two Best Sellers stores have different typical price points. Print books had a median price of $14.99, with the middle 50% priced between $9.99 and $23.55. Kindle eBooks had a median price of $6.99, with the middle 50% between $3.99 and $10.99. Kindle prices cluster at lower levels, while print books generally command higher prices. Both stores had median estimated monthly sales of 6 units.

| Store | Books | P25 | Median | P75 | Median est. sales/mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Print books | 287,153 | $9.99 | $14.99 | $23.55 | 6 |
| Kindle eBooks | 221,584 | $3.99 | $6.99 | $10.99 | 6 |

## Price bands and sales

Print books were most concentrated in the $10–$15 band, which contained 68,356 books, or 23.8% of the store. The $15–$20 band accounted for 18.8%, and $30+ accounted for 16.5%. Median estimated monthly sales were highest in the under $7 band at 9 units, followed by the $7–$10 band at 8 units. The $30+ band had the lowest median at 5 units. Lower-priced print books therefore had higher typical estimated unit sales in this sample, although the figures do not establish why.

Kindle eBooks were most concentrated in the $5–$10 band, which contained 76,835 books, or 34.7% of the store. The $2.99–$5 band contained 63,813 books, representing 28.8%, while $10+ accounted for 26.3%. The under $1 band recorded the highest median estimated monthly sales at 9 units. All other Kindle price bands had a median of 6 units. The largest price band, then, did not have the highest median estimated sales.

## Kindle royalty band and Kindle Unlimited

Of the 221,584 Kindle eBooks in the sample, 63.5% were priced between $2.99 and $9.99, the range eligible for the 70% royalty band. Books under $1 accounted for 6.4% of Kindle eBooks. Kindle Unlimited status was available for 23,004 books, and 44.7% of those were in KU. KU books had median estimated monthly sales of 12 units, compared with 9 units for non-KU books. Median prices were $4.99 for KU books and $8.99 for non-KU books. This sales difference is an association within the Best Sellers sample; it does not show that KU enrollment caused higher sales.

## Print price by page count

Print book prices increase across the page-count categories. The median rises from $9.99 for books under 50 pages to $24.99 for books with 400+ pages. Books with 150–249 pages had a median price of $15.95. The largest listed category, 250–399 pages, contained 32,127 books and had a $17.48 median. Books under 50 pages recorded the highest median estimated monthly sales at 15 units. The 250–399-page category had 11 units, while the other page groups with at least 50 pages recorded median estimated monthly sales between 9 and 10 units. These figures give publishers a useful reference when setting print prices by book length.

## How to price a new book

For a new Kindle eBook, the $5–$10 band offers the largest pool of comparable books, with a store-wide median price of $6.99. Publishers targeting the 70% royalty band can price between $2.99 and $9.99, a range that already includes 63.5% of Kindle books. Fiction has a lower category median of $4.99 and higher median estimated monthly sales of 16 units. How-to guide has a $7.99 median and 6 estimated monthly units. The relevant product type is a better starting point for pricing than the store-wide median alone.

For print books, the $10–$15 band contains the most titles, and the store-wide median is $14.99. Publishers pricing a new 150–249-page book can use the $15.95 page-count median as a reference. For a 250–399-page book, the comparable median is $17.48. Category differences are also worth accounting for: Study guide & reference has a $37.25 print median, while Fiction has a $10.88 median. These figures reflect observed Best Sellers prices; matching a category or page-count median does not guarantee similar sales. Estimated sales are calculated from sales rank using a store-specific rank-to-sales curve, and the figures cover a seven-day period. The sample does not account separately for advertising, publication age, audience size, competition, production costs or book quality. Publishers can use these benchmarks to set an initial price, then assess their own conversion and royalty results when testing price changes.

## Common questions

### What is the median Amazon KDP book price?

For November 2–8, 2026, the median price was $14.99 for Print books and $6.99 for Kindle eBooks. The middle 50% of prices fell between $9.99 and $23.55 for print and between $3.99 and $10.99 for Kindle.

### What Kindle eBook price qualifies for the 70% royalty rate?

The eligible price range in this benchmark is $2.99–$9.99. During November 2–8, 2026, 63.5% of the 221,584 Kindle eBooks fell within that range.

### Do Kindle Unlimited books have higher estimated sales?

In the November 2–8, 2026 Best Sellers sample, KU books had median estimated monthly sales of 12 units, compared with 9 units for non-KU books. Their median price was $4.99, compared with $8.99 for non-KU books. KU status was available for 23,004 books, of which 44.7% were in KU.

### How much should I charge for a print book based on page count?

The November 2–8, 2026 benchmark shows median prices of $9.99 for books under 50 pages, $15.95 for books with 150–249 pages, $17.48 for books with 250–399 pages and $24.99 for books with 400+ pages. These are observed Best Sellers medians. Publishers should also compare prices within their specific product type.

## Method and limits

The report covers the unique books with a price on all Amazon.com Best Sellers lists on 2026-11-08. Prices are the list price shown on the list; monthly sales are estimated from sales rank; page counts come from book pages and Keepa. The Kindle Unlimited share is over Kindle books whose KU status KDPTop has read.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
