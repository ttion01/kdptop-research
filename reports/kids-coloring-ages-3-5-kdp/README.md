1. Kids coloring books for ages 3-5 have a niche score of 78 and rank 4 of 1,605 niches as of November 8, 2026. The lists contain 1,184 books, with estimated sales of 444 units per day.

## How big is the niche

The Kids coloring, ages 3-5 niche contains 1,184 books across Amazon Best Sellers and New Releases lists. These include 393 Best Sellers entries and 865 New Releases entries. Print books account for 1,165 titles, while Kindle eBooks account for 19. Estimated daily sales across the niche total 444 units. The data covers October 10 to November 8, 2026, with sales and sample metrics drawn from October 27 to November 8.

The niche score is 78, placing it 4 of 1,605 niches. The score reflects strong demand, a low review barrier, a steady flow of new releases and substantial indie participation. The estimated 96.6% KDP feasibility indicates that most products in this market can be produced through standard KDP publishing.

## Competition and the review barrier

The niche-level median review count is 4. Among the 1,184 books in the sample, 991 have fewer than 10 reviews, 102 have 10–50, 32 have 50–100, 34 have 100–500 and 25 have more than 500. The sample median is 0 reviews, and 92.3% of books have fewer than 50 reviews. New publishers can enter without competing against large review histories across most of the market, although established titles with substantial review counts remain a factor.

Only 1.9% of sampled books sell 100 or more copies monthly. The monthly sales distribution shows 1,100 books under 30 units, 60 in the 30–100 range, 15 in the 100–300 range, 5 in the 300–1,000 range and 2 above 1,000. The niche-level indie share is 82.1%, compared with 90.2% in the sample. Recent releases account for 64.2% of sampled books, and the niche statistics identify 85 newcomers. Recent publishing activity and low review counts make entry accessible, but monthly sales of 100 or more copies remain concentrated among a small proportion of titles.

## Should you publish in this niche?

Kids coloring, ages 3-5 scores 78 and ranks 4 of 1,605 under the supplied scoring model. Estimated daily sales of 444 units, a median of 4 reviews and an 82.1% niche-level indie share indicate room for independent publishers to enter the market. The supplied data points to several narrower positioning options, including Christian coloring, construction vehicles, trucks and seasonal books. Publishers can investigate these segments rather than relying entirely on broad coloring-book keywords.

Sales concentration is the main constraint. Only 1.9% of sampled books sell 100 or more copies monthly, while 1,100 sell fewer than 30. Low review counts reduce the initial competitive barrier, but they do not guarantee sales. Established licensed titles such as Bluey also hold leading positions. Publishers should distinguish the broad niche's estimated sales from the likely performance of an individual title. The search volumes for Halloween and Christmas coloring books also should not be treated as direct evidence of demand for every preschool coloring concept. The data identifies neither a seasonal peak nor a specific launch window for the overall niche.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-08 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 1,184 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 1,184 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
