## Key findings

1. Kids coloring, ages 3-5, scored 81 for the period ending November 16, 2026, placing 3rd among 1,715 niches. The lists included 1,197 books, with estimated daily sales of 508 units.
2. Amazon.com search demand for Halloween coloring book reached 92,401 searches and 14,442 purchases. Christmas coloring book recorded 24,240 searches and 2,628 purchases.

## How big is the niche

The Kids coloring, ages 3-5 niche contained 1,197 books across Amazon Best Sellers and New Releases lists during the period ending November 16, 2026. Of these, 1,183 were print books and 14 were Kindle eBooks. Estimated daily sales across the niche reached 508 units.

The niche scored 81, ranking 3rd among 1,715 niches. Its score reflects strong demand, a low review barrier, a steady flow of new releases and breakout activity. The data also classifies 97.3% of the books as KDP-feasible. These figures place the niche near the top of the measured market, though the 1,197 listings leave publishers with considerable competition. A focused product angle will matter.

## Competition and the review barrier

Most listings face a low review barrier. The sample median was 0 reviews, with 1,017 books under 10 reviews and 92 in the 10-50 band. Overall, 92.6% had fewer than 50 reviews. Only 1.7% of books sold 100 or more copies per month. The monthly sales distribution included 1,105 books under 30 units, 70 in the 30-100 band, 15 in the 100-300 band, 4 in the 300-1,000 band and 1 above 1,000.

Independently published books accounted for 90.5% of the sample, and 76.7% had been published in the last year. New indie titles continue to enter the market frequently. The low review counts make it relatively easy to enter, but the small proportion of books selling 100 or more copies monthly shows that gaining reviews alone does not translate into substantial sales.

## Should you publish in this niche?

Kids coloring, ages 3-5, offers a substantial market for indie publishers developing focused preschool coloring books. The niche scored 81 and ranked 3rd among 1,715 niches, with estimated daily sales of 508. The niche statistics report a median of 4 reviews, while independently published books account for 90.5% of the book sample. Christian and Christmas themes are the strongest narrower segments in the supplied data, each scoring 74 with low median review counts.

Sales remain concentrated among a small share of titles. Only 1.7% of sampled books sold 100 or more copies monthly, even though 92.6% had fewer than 50 reviews. The $9.99 median price and 72-page median offer useful reference points, but leading books vary in both price and page count. Publishers should read the high niche score as evidence of market activity, not as a sales forecast for an individual title. With 76.7% of sampled books published in the last year, new releases also face an active and frequently changing competitive field.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-16 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 1,197 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 1,197 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and sales-rank history; search volumes are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
