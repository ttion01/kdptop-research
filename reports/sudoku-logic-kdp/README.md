## Competition and the review barrier

The sample has a median of 5 reviews. Of the books examined, 944 have fewer than 10 reviews, 356 have 10-50, 119 have 50-100, 177 have 100-500 and 96 have 500 or more. Books with under 50 reviews account for 76.8% of the sample, giving new entrants a relatively low review barrier. Sales figures present a more difficult picture: only 6.3% of sampled books sell 100 or more copies monthly. Low review counts therefore do not necessarily indicate strong sales potential. The monthly sales distribution includes 1,367 books under 30 units, 206 at 30-100, 66 at 100-300, 30 at 300-1,000 and 9 at 1,000 or more.

Independently published books account for 68.9% of the sample, based on the publisher or author classification. Recent titles also make up a substantial share, with 57.0% of sampled books published in the last year. The niche statistics record 273 newcomers and 210 breakouts, indicating continued new-release activity and showing that some titles gain traction. The top-selling books, however, are largely traditionally published.

## Should you publish in this niche?

Sudoku & logic is worth considering for independent publishers with a clearly differentiated puzzle concept. The niche scores 77 and ranks 9th among 1,716 niches. Estimated daily sales are 5,042, independently published books account for 68.9% of the sample, and 76.8% of sampled books have fewer than 50 reviews. Christmas sudoku & logic and Sudoku & logic for stress relief offer narrower markets to investigate, with scores of 80 and 70 respectively.

The main concern is the difference between the relatively accessible review barrier and the sales figures. Only 6.3% of sampled books sell 100 or more copies monthly, while 1,367 sell fewer than 30. The leading titles also demonstrate the market position of established mystery-puzzle brands such as Murdoku and Murdle. New publishers need a distinctive format and a clear reason for readers to choose their book over these established series. The data covers October 17 to November 15, 2026, with underlying collection dates from October 27 to November 15, 2026. It does not establish a seasonal peak or identify a specific optimal publication month.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-15 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 1,690 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 1,692 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
