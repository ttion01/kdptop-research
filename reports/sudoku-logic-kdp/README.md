## Key findings

1. Sudoku & logic ranked 9th among 1,715 Amazon KDP niches as of November 16, 2026, with a niche score of 77 and 1,705 books listed across Best Sellers and New Releases.
2. Amazon.com Sudoku & logic listings recorded estimated daily sales of 5,051 units as of November 16, 2026, showing substantial existing demand across the niche.
3. The Sudoku & logic sample had a median of 5 reviews, with 76.6% of books having fewer than 50 reviews as of November 16, 2026. This leaves independent publishers room to compete with newer titles.

## How big is the niche

The Sudoku & logic niche contains 1,705 books across Amazon Best Sellers and New Releases lists: 1,220 print books and 485 Kindle eBooks. Estimated daily sales reach 5,051 units. The niche scores 77 out of 100, placing it 9th among 1,715 niches. Its 211 breakouts and 280 newcomers point to ongoing activity, with new titles continuing to enter the market.

The 98.8% KDP feasibility score indicates that most products in this niche are suitable for independent print publishing. Publishers can approach the market through several established formats, including conventional Sudoku collections and murder mystery logic puzzles.

## Competition and the review barrier

The sample has a median review count of 5. A total of 960 books have fewer than 10 reviews, and 76.6% have fewer than 50. Only 6.2% sell 100 or more copies monthly, while 1,380 books sell fewer than 30 monthly units. New publishers can enter without matching the review counts of leading titles, but consistent sales remain concentrated among a relatively small share of listings.

Independently published books account for 69.4% of the sample, and 57.4% were published within the last year. The 280 newcomers and 211 breakouts show that new titles can find an audience. However, the 1,705-book market also includes substantial competition from established publishers and series.

## Should you publish in this niche?

Sudoku & logic offers a workable opportunity for publishers who can create a distinctive puzzle format or serve a more specific audience. Its score of 77 and rank of 9th among 1,715 niches justify further research. The 69.4% indie share and median of 5 reviews also indicate that independent publishers can enter without matching the review counts of established leaders. Christmas sudoku & logic and Sudoku & logic for stress relief are two narrower options, with scores of 81 and 69 respectively.

Sales concentration is the main concern. Only 6.2% of sampled books sell 100 or more copies monthly, despite estimated daily niche sales of 5,051. Leading murder mystery titles record strong estimated sales, but their review counts and established series make direct competition difficult. The number of recent releases also warrants attention. Publishers should assess search volume alongside purchase data rather than treating searches as proof of sales potential. The data does not identify a seasonal peak month for the overall niche, so it cannot establish a precise launch window.

## Method and limits

The niche's books are those on an Amazon.com Best Sellers or New Releases list in the three days to 2026-11-16 whose AI classification falls in this niche: 1,705 books. The review, sales and price distributions use the 1,705 of them KDPTop has full details for; sales are estimated from sales rank.

List data comes from KDPTop's daily crawl of every Amazon.com Books and Kindle Best Sellers and New Releases list; book details come from the list cards, book pages and Keepa; search volumes come from a third-party keyword tool and are estimates. Adult content categories are excluded.

Limits: lists reflect rank, not actual sales, and every sales figure is estimated from rank; books KDPTop has not seen are not counted.
